# Admin UI Upgrade Walkthrough

I have completely redesigned the Admin panel with a **Premium Light Theme** that perfectly matches the "The Marwadi" frontend brand!

## What Changed

### 1. Theming & Colors
- **Cream / Off-White Background**: The harsh grey is gone. Replaced with a premium, soft cream background (`#fdfbf7`) that gives a natural, organic feel suited for dry fruits and spices.
- **Maroon & Gold**: Primary elements like the sidebar titles, active states, and buttons now use the deep maroon (`#5c0b0b`) and gold (`#d4af37`) color palette.

### 2. Typography
- **Playfair Display**: Brought in the elegant serif font from the frontend for all Admin headers and the brand logo to make the dashboard feel consistent with the main website.
- **Inter**: Upgraded the data table and form text for supreme readability.

### 3. Polish & Effects
- **Floating Glassmorphism**: The top navigation bar now floats and blurs the content beneath it when scrolling.
- **Shadows & Rounding**: Cards and modals now feature very soft, modern drop-shadows with perfectly rounded corners.
- **Micro-animations**: Added a smooth fade-in animation when switching tabs, and premium hover effects on buttons and table rows (rows gently scale and highlight on hover).

Check your Admin Panel now—it should look like a state-of-the-art SaaS application!
